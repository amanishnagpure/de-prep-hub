## Phase 1 — SQL (weeks 1–3)

The core of DE interviews. Use **SQL Lab** end to end.

| Week | Focus | Target |
|------|--------|--------|
| 1 | [Notes](/sql/notes) Easy + Medium | 3 chapters read |
| 2 | [Practice](/sql/practice) | 25 problems solved |
| 3 | [LeetCode](/sql/leetcode) must-do + [Interview](/sql/interview) flashcards | 15 LC confident, 25 Q&A |

Finish with a [45-min mock](/sql/mock).

## Phase 2 — System design (week 4)

## Phase 5 — Interview prep & mock loop (week 8+)

Use **Interview Prep Lab** for behavioral and STAR practice.

Repeat weekly until interviews:

1. SQL mock session
2. Review weak areas on [Overview](/sql)
3. Drill LC + flashcards marked *unsure*
4. One [system-design case](/system-design/practice) out loud
5. Two [STAR prompts](/interview-prep/practice) recorded
