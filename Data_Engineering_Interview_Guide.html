General / Experience / Behavioral Tips

These recurred constantly (total experience, cloud experience, number of projects, comfort level in ADF/Databricks/SQL, role and responsibilities, day-to-day activities, domain of project, team handling, client communication across time zones, challenges faced, preparing for a new project/client call).

  • Total/cloud experience, project count, comfort ratings: answer honestly and specifically (e.g., “2 years total, 1.5 years on Azure, 7/10 in ADF, 6/10 in Databricks”).
  • Role & responsibilities: describe the pipeline/data flow you owned end-to-end (ingestion → transformation → load), tools used, and your specific contribution.
  • Domain: name the business domain (banking, retail, healthcare, etc.) and briefly what data you handled.
  • Team handling: if you led/mentored, describe team size, how you split work, and how you unblocked members.
  • Client communication across time zones: mention overlap hours, async updates via email/Teams, and status reporting cadence.
  • Challenges faced: use a STAR format — Situation, Task, Action, Result — picking a real technical issue (e.g., a slow pipeline, schema drift, data skew) and how you diagnosed and fixed it.
  • Preparing for a new project as lead: requirement gathering, architecture design, task breakdown/estimation, environment/access setup, defining coding standards, and a communication/status plan before the client call.
